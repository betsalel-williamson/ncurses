# References

> Adapted from *NCURSES Programming HOWTO*, by Pradeep Padala.

Where to read more about ncurses:

- **The ncurses manual pages.** They're the complete reference for the library, and this site has [all of them](https://betsalel-williamson.github.io/ncurses/man/3x/ncurses/), starting from `ncurses(3X)`.
- **The ncurses FAQ**, by Thomas E. Dickey, at [invisible-island.net/ncurses/ncurses.faq.html](https://invisible-island.net/ncurses/ncurses.faq.html).
- ***Writing Programs with NCURSES***, by Eric S. Raymond and Zeyd M. Ben-Halim. The HOWTO's author was inspired by it, and the HOWTO follows its structure. Parts of it are out of date. This site's guides include it, corrected: the [initialization, wrapup and debugging](../client-library/init-and-debugging/index.md) and [hints, tips and compatibility](../client-library/hints/index.md) guides come from its later sections, and other guides credit it where they draw on it. The original is at [invisible-island.net/ncurses/ncurses-intro.html](https://invisible-island.net/ncurses/ncurses-intro.html).
