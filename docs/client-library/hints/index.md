# Hints, tips and compatibility

The manual pages are a complete reference for ncurses. These pages discuss useful methods that may not be obvious from the manual page descriptions, the places where ncurses behaves differently from older curses libraries, and how ncurses conforms to the X/Open Curses standard. They come from the Hints, Tips, and Tricks section of *Writing Programs with NCURSES*, by Eric S. Raymond and Zeyd M. Ben-Halim, and the two sections after it.
