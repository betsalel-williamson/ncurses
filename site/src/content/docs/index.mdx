## Written by the ncurses authors

ncurses and its documentation are the work of its maintainer and contributors. All the text on this site is theirs.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Maintainer">
    **Thomas E. Dickey** has maintained ncurses since 1.9.9e and wrote or revised most of the manual pages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Principal authors">
    **Juergen Pfeifer**, **Eric S. Raymond**, **Alexander V. Lukyanov**, **G. Branden Robinson**, **Philippe Blain**, **Sven Verdoolaege** and **Nicolas Boulenguez**, in the order of their contributions in the project's AUTHORS file.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Originators">
    **Pavel Curtis** originated ncurses, and **Zeyd M. Ben-Halim** was its original maintainer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Guides">
    *Writing Programs with NCURSES* by **Eric S. Raymond** and **Zeyd M. Ben-Halim**, *A Hacker's Guide to NCURSES* by **Eric S. Raymond**, and the *NCURSES Programming HOWTO* by **Pradeep Padala**.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Many more people contributed patches, reports and analysis. The project's NEWS file records each of them. See [Credits](credits/) for the full list and sources.
